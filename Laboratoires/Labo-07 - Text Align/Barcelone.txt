Your trip to Barcelona

Your flight information
You will have the opportunity to choose your airline and flight times when making your reservation.
This flight may include one or more stopovers. A stopover may be technical (without a change of aircraft) or transit (with a change of aircraft).

Baggage and in-flight services
Baggage, meals, and other onboard services on certain flights may incur additional charges.
You will find out whether your airfare includes checked baggage when you select your airline and flight schedule.

If your fare does not include baggage, we invite you to visit the airline website before your departure, using your transport booking reference, to check the exact fee and purchase any additional baggage.
If you choose a low-cost airline, you will be able to add your baggage during the booking process.

For domestic flights, airlines do not always offer the option to pre-book baggage; it may therefore be payable directly at the airport check-in counter.

For certain airlines, the leg from a provincial city to Paris, or from Brussels to Paris, may be operated by train.
This information is displayed when you click on Details during your selection.

To take the train, you must have your electronic train ticket (e-ticket), which can be downloaded between 72 hours and 30 hours before your trip.
Once you have your e-ticket, you can go directly to your train on the day of departure. You can also collect your e-ticket from SNCF self-service kiosks.

You will receive all information regarding check-in for your flights after you complete your booking.

Transfers

Round-trip transfers between the airport and the hotel are not included in our offers but are available as shared or private transfers, as an optional extra for flight-inclusive packages.

Car rental

To enjoy full freedom of movement and travel at your own pace, we offer car rental during the booking process.
Thanks to your travel agency, you can benefit from special conditions for your getaways around the world, behind the wheel of your favorite car!

This is an optional extra, available only with packages that include transportation, and subject to the opening hours of BSP partner agency at your arrival time.

To rent a vehicle, you must hold a valid driver license recognized in the country of rental (a license valid within the European Union or an international driving permit).
